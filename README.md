Retail / E-commerce Sales Analysis
Overview
This project analyzes retail and e-commerce sales data to identify revenue trends, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and operational inefficiencies. The analysis focuses on discounts, competition intensity, inventory pressure, and regional sales performance.

Dataset Information
- Total Records: 27,926 sales records
- Industry: Retail / E-commerce
- Focus Area: Revenue & Business Growth Analysis

Objectives
- Identify top-performing product categories
- Analyze impact of discounts on profitability
- Study regional sales performance
- Improve inventory and pricing strategies

Key Insights
- Top categories generated revenue in crores
- Medium discounts improved sales performance
- High competition intensity reduced average revenue
- Inventory pressure affected operational efficiency
- Suggested strategies could improve sales growth by 10–18%

Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- SQL
- MySQL
- Power BI
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

Project Workflow
1. Data Cleaning & Preparation
2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
3. SQL-Based Business Queries
4. Revenue & Sales Analysis
5. Dashboard & Business Insights

Business Impact
- Improved pricing and discount strategy understanding
- Better inventory management insights
- Enhanced regional sales analysis
- Supported data-driven business decisions
